# Wien Kultur — Einzelförderung darstellende Kunst

**Deadline 15 September 2026** · natural person · max **€30,000** · `theater@ma07.wien.gv.at`
Verified 2026-08-25 against the [Förderrichtlinien](https://www.wien.gv.at/kultur/abteilung/foerderungen/richtlinie-theater.html).
Apply here **before** BMWKMS on 30 September: the federal grant is *subsidiär* to regional funding.

The ledger is the part most applications don't have. Twenty sources with `verified` / `probable` / `chosen` / `open` statuses, and a named exception to the theorem, is documented research method — not a statement of intent.

## What only you can supply

- Date of birth, bank details, **Meldebestätigung** (current Vienna residence certificate)
- **Public funding received in the last three years** — the form asks explicitly
- The requested amount (≤ €30,000)
- The **Excel Kalkulation** — official template; different form under/over €5,001
- Venue and dates — not required for a standard Einzelförderung, but they strengthen it

## Budget lines the project already names

[production.md#production](../docs/production.md) lists what the process needs. Each is a budget line:

1. Studio where objects can stay in place between sessions — *weeks × rate*
2. Regular outside eye or dramaturg — *fee × sessions*
3. Four audience tests of increasing size — *venue, invigilation*
4. Early collaboration with lighting and sound — *design fees*
5. Video and still documentation of late-stage runs — *day rate*
6. Own performer fee — *twelve weeks*

Costing those six is most of the Kalkulation.

## Two things to settle first

- **Enrolment.** If you enrol before applying, check the BMWKMS exclusion of *"sich in Ausbildung befindende Personen"* — it reads as aimed at drama-school students, not professionals doing a doctorate, but confirm before it costs a deadline. Wien Kultur has no equivalent clause.
- **The three-performance question.** BMWKMS requires ≥3 performances; Wien Kultur requires them only for Wiederaufnahme. Plan the dates once, satisfying both.
