# The Work

## What is already known {#concept}

Solo Titania Chaos is being developed as a physical, wordless clown solo around **solitude / being alone**.

The field includes solitude, loneliness, isolation, connection, privacy, the private self, being alone in the presence of others, wanting contact and avoiding it.

Titania does not need to explain or represent these ideas. The material begins with concrete actions, needs, problems, objects, space, rhythm, failure and the audience.

Meaning may emerge from what happens. It does not have to be decided in advance.

### Built from playable material

The work will be developed from **playable building blocks rather than a predetermined sequence of scenes**.

This does not determine the final form. A fixed dramaturgy may eventually emerge. For now, structure follows what survives rehearsal rather than preceding it.

### The audience is already there

Audience contact is fundamental to Titania's clown practice.

That creates a particular problem for a solo about being alone: Titania may be alone on stage, but she is performing in the presence of other people.

What happens to that relationship — whether she seeks it, avoids it, needs it, forgets it or loses it — remains to be discovered.

## The central contradiction {#contradiction}

**Can a clown investigate being alone when clown exists through relationship?**

Clown happens through contact. Something occurs, the clown registers it, the audience registers the clown, and the event becomes shared.

Solitude complicates that relationship.

An audience can watch Titania being alone — but by watching her, they are also there with her.

A laugh makes their presence impossible to ignore.

The question is therefore not simply how to **depict** solitude, but what can happen to presence, absence and connection in a room shared by clown and audience.

There is no answer yet.

## Questions for the studio {#questions}

The project currently has questions rather than scenes.

They are not a hidden synopsis. They are starting points for physical investigation.

<div class="studio-questions">
  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>Can Titania discover the audience?</h3>
    <p>Can noticing the people in the room become an event rather than something taken for granted?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>Can Titania stop needing the audience?</h3>
    <p>Can something else become sufficiently absorbing that her relationship with the audience changes or temporarily disappears?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>Can something become company?</h3>
    <p>Can an object, space, light, sound or repeated action begin to function as company — and when does playing <em>as if</em> become a relationship for the clown?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>What happens when something disappears?</h3>
    <p>What kinds of absence actually change Titania's behaviour?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>What does failure reveal?</h3>
    <p>Failure already belongs to Titania's clown practice. When something is wanted, attempted and fails, could that witnessed failure become one of the basic units from which material grows?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>What can remain private?</h3>
    <p>Can there be privacy on stage while an audience is watching, and what happens when something private becomes visible?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>What does wordlessness do?</h3>
    <p>Wordlessness is not silence. What becomes possible when Titania cannot explain herself and meaning has to travel through body, rhythm, breath, objects, space, light and the audience?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="studio-question">
    <h3>Where does comedy have to remain?</h3>
    <p>A piece investigating solitude may move through pleasure, discomfort, quietness or sadness, but Titania remains a clown. How far can comedy withdraw without the clown disappearing with it?</p>
  </section>
</div>

## The research field {#research}

Research around the project brings together different ways of thinking about being alone: **solitude, loneliness, isolation, chosen and imposed aloneness, privacy, absence, connection and being alone in the presence of others.**

These distinctions are not a proposed dramaturgy.

They are there to help us notice differences that might otherwise look the same.

Research may sharpen a question, expose a contradiction or give a name to something that happens in rehearsal. It does not determine what Titania should do.

**The research is there to sharpen observation, not to write the piece.**

## What remains unknown {#unknown}

A great deal — deliberately.

**Why this subject now?**

I do not yet have an answer that I trust.

It is also not yet known what material will survive rehearsal, what overall structure will emerge, which objects will matter, how much direct audience interaction the work will need, or what the ending will be.

Even the building-block approach does not determine the final form. The finished solo may eventually become tightly structured.

These are not gaps that need to be filled on paper.

They are reasons to rehearse.

## Into the studio {#studio}

The next step is not to complete the dramaturgy on paper.

It is to put the questions into the room.

The studio begins with concrete tasks, materials, objects, space and audiences. Ideas that generate playable behaviour can stay. Ideas that remain only ideas have not yet earned their place.

What emerges may confirm the research, contradict it or make parts of it irrelevant.

**That is the point.**
