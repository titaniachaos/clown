# Audience test — information and consent

**Draft.** Your institution's ethics committee will have a template that
supersedes this. Use it to start the conversation, not to replace their form.

---

## What this is

You are invited to watch a work-in-progress showing of *Solo Titania Chaos*, a
wordless clown solo, and to be part of the room while it is tested.

The performance is being made as practice-based research. What the research
needs is not your opinion but what happens in the room: when people laugh, when
they go quiet, where attention goes.

## What is recorded

- **Observations of the room as a whole** — laughter, silence, timing, movement.
  These are written as counts and notes. **No one is named or described
  individually.**
- **Video and stills of the performance**, for the artist's own study of timing
  and, later, for short extracts shown to programmers and funders. The camera
  faces the stage. Audience members may be audible, and may be visible at the
  edges of frame.

## What is not recorded

No names, no contact details, no personal information of any kind is collected.

## If you would rather not be filmed

**Seats in the back row on the left are outside the camera frame.** Sit there
and you will not appear. Nothing else changes and nothing is lost — you are as
useful to the research there as anywhere else.

## How long the material is kept

Working footage is kept until the piece premieres and is then deleted. Any
extract shown publicly is agreed separately and is limited to material where no
audience member is identifiable.

## Withdrawing

You can leave at any time, without explanation. If you decide afterwards that
you would rather not be in the footage, contact
**agent@tatianapetkova.com** and it will be removed.
